# Week 11: Week 11: Emerging Paradigms

Lesson 1: We study the emerging area of stream processing, touching on key design aspects of Apache Storm.  
Lesson 2: We study how enormous graphs can be processed in clouds. 
Lesson 3: We study various types of networks/graphs that are both natural and artificial, and their surprising commonalities.  
Lesson 4: This module presents classical scheduling algorithms that have been used in operating systems since the inception of computers. We then cover two popular scheduling algorithms for Hadoop.
