# Instructions for Taking the Prerequisite Quiz

- Coursera: https://www.coursera.org/learn/cs-425/supplement/TaaPi/instructions-for-taking-the-prerequisite-quiz
- Lesson: Basic Computer Science Fundamentals

The prerequisite quiz tests your basic ability to apply some of the background materials we consider important for this course. It also tests your ability to perform basic mathematical calculations, which you will need to complete some of the quiz questions in this course.

**_If you do not score at least a 90% on this quiz, then the Cloud Computing Concepts course may not be for you!_**

If you score less than 100%, please thoroughly review the material on the questions that you missed so you are well-prepared.
