## Course Communication

When you are in need of help, please follow the two simple steps below to reach out to us.

-

[**Piazza Forum**](https://piazza.com/illinois/fall2026/cs425ece428) - this is your go-to place to ask course content related questions for clarification on lecture videos, and any course assignments. Before you post any question, check if someone has already asked your question. Instructor and TA will attempt to answer questions within 24 - 48 hours after questions are posted. It does not mean that you should post questions at the last minute, e.g., 10 minutes before the deadline. It is highly recommended to check the Piazza forum at least twice a day.
-

**cs-425-staff@mx.uillinois.edu** - In cases where your technical questions did not get answered on Piazza, you can use the staff email list to email the instructor and course TA. You can also inquire for any private issues to the staff email list. Are you sure you've waited long enough for an answer on Piazza (24 - 48 hours is typical), or that your question is private?
-

**mcs-support@illinois.edu** - this is your go-to place for non-content related, administrative questions you may have for course staff, the program and technical issues with the platform . We are committed to answering your questions with 24 - 48 hours during the working week.

## Course Help Resources

### For Course Content Issues and Discussions

If you have questions about any lecture videos or assignments, the course Piazza forum is your first go-to place. You can search if someone has already posted a similar question. If not, you can post your own thread in the forum.

For MCS-DS Program Support

The MCS-DS Support Team of teaching and operational specialists from both the University of Illinois and Coursera are dedicated to providing you with program support and to help with any question you have from course enrollment to letters of course completion. You can reach this team at mcsds-support@illinois.edu.

### For Coursera Platform Urgent Technical Support

If you need to speak to someone for urgent technical support, you are very welcome to use our chat support. This support is offered 24 hours a day 7 days per week. As a degree student, you can access chat support on _any_ page of the [Course Help Center](https://learner.coursera.help).

Remember that Coursera chat support does not always know that you are a degree student. Please **let them know that** **you are a degree student for the MCS-DS program** as soon as you start a chat. That way we can ensure that your problem is solved as quickly as possible.

### For Disability Accommodations

To obtain disability-related academic adjustments and/or auxiliary aids, students should contact both the instructor and the Disability Resources and Educational Services (DRES) as soon as possible. You can contact DRES at 1207 S. Oak Street, Champaign, (217) 333-1970, or via email at disability@illinois.edu. You can also visit the [DRES homepage](http://disability.illinois.edu/).
