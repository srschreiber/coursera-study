## Late Policy

-

Where not specified, the assignment is due at 11:59 PM Central Time. ([Time Zone Converter](http://www.thetimezoneconverter.com/))
-

The hard deadline is the deadline after which you will receive 0 points regardless of how well you did on the assignment. No late submissions will be accepted except under extremely rare non-academic circumstances (which usually require approval from the Dean's office).
-

No assignment will be accepted after the last day of class.
-

You can also see when each assignment is due by clicking the **Assignments **tab on the left menu bar.

## Course Withdrawn and Refund

For course withdrawn-related questions, please refer to the Academic Calendars at [http://registrar.illinois.edu/academic-calendars](http://registrar.illinois.edu/academic-calendars) and select _Fall_ _2026_ on that page.

This fall course is a GRAD POT (Part of Term) 1, AKA, a full 16-week semester course.

For course refund information, please refer to the Office of the Registrar refunds website: [http://registrar.illinois.edu/refunds](http://registrar.illinois.edu/refunds) and select _Fall 2026_ term. You can also refer to this website for the pro-rate refund schedule if you plan to withdraw from the course after the first day of classes for any given semester.
