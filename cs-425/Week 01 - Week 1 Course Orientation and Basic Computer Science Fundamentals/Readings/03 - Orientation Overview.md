# Orientation

## Overview

This course orientation prepares you for CS 425: Distributed Systems. In the course orientation, you will learn what the course is about by reading the course syllabus. You will also become familiar with the overall structure of the course, deadlines, policies, and course communication. You will have the chance to introduce yourself to your classmates and get to know each other better.

In addition, the orientation includes an introductory lecture video on basic computer science fundamentals. Once you finish the introductory lecture video, please take the Prerequisite Quiz, which is designed to test whether you have the necessary background for this course. **If you do not score at least 90% on the Prerequisite Quiz, then this course may not be for you.**

## Time

The course orientation begins from week 1 on the course, together with the basic computer science fundamentals lesson. The orientation should take **approximately 3 hours** of dedicated time to complete.
