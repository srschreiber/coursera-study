**Topics:** All material of C3 Part 1 (Weeks 1-7)

## Instructions

-

Only **two** attempts allowed for the Coursera final exam. The grade will be the highest score from the two attempts.
-

 The part 1 Coursera final exam must be done individually.
-

 For multiple choice, please note whether the question says there are MULTIPLE correct answers. For questions with MULTIPLE answers, you have to specify ALL correct answers and ONLY the correct answers.
-

There are 30 questions total.

## Evaluation

Each question is worth 1 point in Coursera. But the point value will be scaled down to 0.25 pts per question to calculate the grade of the exam in this course.
