# Week 2: Week 2: Introduction to Clouds, MapReduce 

Lesson 1: Cloud computing is the latest in a long generation of distributed systems. This module first presents a brief history of distributed computing. We then look at differences and similarities between cloud computing and previous generations of distributed systems, and aspects that make cloud computing unique. Lesson 2: This module motivates the study of clouds as the study of distributed systems, and defines the latter. This course henceforth focuses on distributed systems concepts that underlie today’s clouds. Lesson 3: This module teaches MapReduce, the popular paradigm for writing parallel programs to process large datasets. We cover several examples in pseudocode (along with a wee bit of code), and then look at how Hadoop schedules jobs. The lecture is oriented around concepts, rather than teaching you how to code.
